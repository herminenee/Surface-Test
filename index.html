Advancing and receding contact angles → physics-based sliding-angle estimate. Default droplet volume: 20 µL.

Experimental inputs

Model assumptions

Spherical-cap contact-line geometry, quasi-static force balance, g = 10 m/s² to match the original Surface-Test script. This is a predictive estimate, not a fitted or experimentally validated model. Contact-line diameter is computed internally, not a fixed geometric value. Hysteresis is used internally but not shown as a standalone measurement.
